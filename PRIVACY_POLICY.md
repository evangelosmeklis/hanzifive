# Privacy Policy

**HanziFive** (汉字5)

*Last updated: February 7, 2026*

## Overview

HanziFive is a Chinese character learning app that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device** only:

- **Study progress** (review states, spaced repetition data)
- **App settings** (daily goal, reminders, display preferences)
- **Custom decks** (cards you import)

No data ever leaves your device.

## Data Collection

We do **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Crash reports

## Third-Party Services

HanziFive does **not** integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Notifications

If you enable study reminders, the app uses local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

## Data Deletion

All app data can be removed by deleting the app from your device. Since no data is stored externally, deletion is immediate and complete.

## Children's Privacy

HanziFive does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected in this document with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/evangelosmeklis/hanzifive).
