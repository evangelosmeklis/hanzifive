# Promotional Text
Learn Chinese characters with short, focused study sessions built around the HSK levels. HanziFive makes daily practice simple with spaced repetition, streaks, and a clean interface that keeps you moving.

# Description
HanziFive is a minimal HSK flashcard app designed to help you build a daily study habit. Practice the most common Chinese characters, review what’s due, and track your progress as you move through the levels.

Highlights
- Spaced repetition that adapts to your recall
- Fast study sessions with tap-to-reveal cards
- Reverse mode to strengthen recall from English to Hanzi
- Random practice for quick refreshers
- Streaks, daily goals, and progress tracking

Whether you’re just getting started or brushing up your foundation, HanziFive keeps learning focused, calm, and effective.
